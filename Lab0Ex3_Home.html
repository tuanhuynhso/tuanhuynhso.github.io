Welcome to Café Dulcinea

Welcome to Café Dulcinea, La Manchaland’s premier destination for those who need caffeine, need comfort or simply need to dramatically stare into the middle distance like a tragic protagonist.

Curious what’s brewing today? Take a look at our full menu and find the perfect potion for your quest.

See the menu →

Nestled conveniently between a souvenir shop that definitely isn’t cursed and a windmill that definitely isn’t a boss fight, our café offers a warm refuge from the harsh realities of the City — or at least a nice place to pretend those realities are part of your character development arc.

Our beans are roasted with the same dedication knights once reserved for duels, and our pastries are baked fresh daily (unless the ovens decide to be existential again). Whether you’re here to plan your next impossible quest, finish paperwork you’ve been avoiding, or just sip something warm while contemplating your life decisions, we’ve got you covered.

So come on in, grab a cup of our finest brew, and let the ambiance of Café Dulcinea inspire you to greatness — or at least to a really good Instagram post.
